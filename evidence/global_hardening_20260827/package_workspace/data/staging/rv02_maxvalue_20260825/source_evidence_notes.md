
## Fresh retrieval for pasted_content_100 / pasted_content_101 continuation

The source pages for Ayodhya Kanda Sargas 102–109 were retrieved from StotraNidhi IAST pages on 2026-08-25. Sarga 102 (`nivāpadānam`) displays verses 1–49 and documents Dasaratha's death being reported, mourning, and Rama's water rite at Mandakini. Sarga 103 (`mātr̥darśanam`) displays verses 1–32 and documents the royal mothers' arrival and meeting with Rama, Lakshmana, Sita, and Bharata. Sarga 104 (`rāmabharatasaṁvādaḥ`) displays verses 1–27 and documents Bharata's request that Rama return and Rama's explanation of the division assigned by Dasaratha. Sarga 105 (`rāmavākyam`) displays verses 1–46 and records Bharata's renewed appeal and Rama's teaching on impermanence, duty, and obedience to the father's command. Sarga 106 (`bharatavacanam`) displays verses 1–33 and a colophon, but the visible numbering jumps from 33 to 35; this is retained as a VARIANT_NOTE and is not silently corrected. Sarga 107 (`rāmaprativacanam`) displays verses 1–19 and Rama's response to Bharata, including the succession arrangement and the brothers' mutual duties. Sarga 108 (`jābālivākyam`) displays verses 1–18 and records Jabali's materialist argument to persuade Rama to return. Sarga 109 (`satyapraśaṁsā`) displays verses 1–39 and records Rama's defense of truth, dharma, and the duty to uphold Dasaratha's command.
