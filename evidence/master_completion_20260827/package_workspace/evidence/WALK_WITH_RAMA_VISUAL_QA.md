# Walk with Rama visual QA

Generated 2026-08-27 after capturing the new route and the existing Home gateway at 390×844 and 1280×720.

## Desktop

The Walk with Rama hero establishes the intended deep-navy, Ayodhya-gold, Rama-blue atmosphere with a lightweight dawn glow, bow outline, river-line motif, and symbolic silhouette. The situation selector reads as a clear interaction band, while the reflection and trust/source cards form a strong two-column evidence-first composition. The eight chapter cards preserve the calm visual rhythm without relying on video or heavy imagery.

## Mobile

At 390px the hero, situation list, reflection, evidence panel, and chapters stack cleanly within the narrow viewport. The 11 situation controls remain large enough for touch, selected state is visible, and the source locator remains readable without horizontal overflow. The footer remains reachable after the long-form experience.

## Accessibility and safety observations

The selected situation is exposed with `aria-pressed`, the response region uses `aria-live=polite`, the symbolic portal has an accessible image label, the audio control is visibly off by default, and reduced-motion rules remove hover transforms. The experience explicitly says it is a source-grounded reflection inspired by Rama's words and actions and does not claim that Rama is literally speaking through the AI. Evidence selection filters identifiers beginning with `STAGING-` before rendering source details.
