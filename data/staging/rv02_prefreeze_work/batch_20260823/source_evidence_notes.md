
## Fresh retrieval 2026-08-23

The accessible StotraNidhi IAST pages were re-retrieved for Sargas 78–89. Each page identifies Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, and an explicit Sarga colophon. The pages provide source text but are an accessible textual presentation, not a final critical-edition certification; independent-edition cross-check remains pending.

- Sarga 78, `kubjāvikṣēpaḥ`, verses 1–26: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-78-in-english/ . Evidence: Shatrughna rebukes Manthara after Rama's exile; Bharata intervenes and Manthara is released.
- Sarga 79, `sacivaprārthanāpratiṣēdhaḥ`, verses 1–17: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-79-in-english/ . Evidence: ministers ask Bharata to accept the kingdom; he affirms Rama's seniority, refuses, and orders preparations to seek Rama.
- Sarga 80, `mārgasaṁskāraḥ`, verses 1–22: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-80-in-english/ . Evidence: specialists clear and construct the route, camps, water facilities, and settlements toward the Ganga.
- Sarga 81, `sabhāstānam`, verses 1–16: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-81-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata rejects the royal title; Vasistha summons the assembly.
- Sarga 82, `sēnāprasthāpanam`, verses 1–32: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-82-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata restates Rama's seniority and directs Sumantra and the forces to prepare the expedition.
- Sarga 83, `bharatavanaprasthānam`, verses 1–26: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-83-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata departs with ministers, priests, army, royal mothers, citizens, and attendants, reaching the Ganga near Shringaverapura.
- Sarga 84, `guhāgamanam`, verses 1–18: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-84-in-english/ . Evidence: Guha initially assesses the army cautiously, guards the river and boats, and approaches Bharata with provisions.
- Sarga 85, `guhasamāgamaḥ`, verses 1–22: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-85-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata explains that he seeks Rama's return, not harm; Guha responds with cooperation.
- Sarga 86, `guhavākyam`, verses 1–25: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-86-in-english/ . Evidence: Guha recounts Lakshmana's vigilance and Rama and Sita's crossing of the Ganga.
- Sarga 87, `rāmaśayanādipraśnaḥ`, displayed numbering includes a gap at 4: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-87-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata asks about Rama's first night; Guha describes the food refusal, fast, bedding, and vigil. The displayed numbering gap is retained as a variant note.
- Sarga 88, `śayyānuvīkṣaṇam`, verses 1–30: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-88-in-english/ . Evidence: Bharata examines the sleeping place and resolves to share austerity while seeking Rama.
- Sarga 89, `gaṅgātaraṇam`, verses 1–23: https://stotranidhi.com/en/ayodhya-kanda-sarga-89-in-english/ . Evidence: Guha arranges the Ganga crossing and Bharata proceeds toward Prayaga and Bharadvaja's hermitage.
